About NourishHub

Clossing The Loop

NourishHub is a social enterprise dedicated to tackling two pressing issues in India: food waste and unemployment. It operates through a smart, digital platform that connects food donors—such as restaurants, supermarkets, and households—with non-profit organizations and communities in need. The goal is to recover surplus, edible food and redirect it to the underprivileged, thereby reducing hunger and preventing unnecessary waste.
