How It Works

Our simple three-step process makes food donation and distribution seamless for everyone involved.

1

Post Donation

Donors list available food items with details like quantity, location, and pickup time.

2

Match & Notify

Our system matches donations with nearby recipients and notifies them instantly.

3

Collect & Distribute

Recipients collect the food or our delivery partners transport it to those in need.
